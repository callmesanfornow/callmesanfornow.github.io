I am a PhD researcher in multilingual speech and language AI at Télécom SudParis, Institut Polytechnique de Paris. I help platforms and organisations detect and audit harmful content across languages, including low-resource and code-mixed ones, using pre-trained speech and text models.

What I can help with
===
- **Moderation pipelines for many languages:** building speech and text classifiers for abusive and hateful content in multilingual and low-resource settings.
- **Choosing the right architecture:** deciding whether to transcribe voice content before classifying it, or to work on the audio directly.
- **Fairness and error audits:** checking error rates per language and per group, so that a good average score does not hide who bears the errors.
- **New languages with little data:** getting a new language working with tens, not thousands, of labelled examples.

Selected results
===
- Few-shot detection of abusive speech across **10 Indian languages**, with **50–200 labelled examples per language** ([COLING 2025](/publication/sankaran2025towards)).
- A simple classifier on frozen CLAP audio embeddings comes **within 1–3 points of full supervision** and far outperforms zero-shot prompting ([preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.09094)).
- A gender-stereotype classifier for rhymes and poems with **97% accuracy**, plus LLM rewrites benchmarked against educators' corrections ([LREC-COLING 2024](/publication/sankaran2024revisiting)).

Research
===
My research looks at how pre-trained speech and text representations can help in studying abusive language: what they capture about language, speakers and bias, and how well they transfer to languages they were never trained on. I focus on hate and offensive content moderation in low-resource and code-mixed/code-switched languages, and I also work on computational digital humanities, such as code-switching in K-pop and its link to chart success.

I am supervised by [Noel Crespi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=z_-5wSkAAAAJ) and [Reza Farahbakhsh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=HukBnBlWTE0C&) at the Data Intelligence and Communication Engineering Lab, and I hold a fellowship from the Indo-French Centre for the Promotion of Advanced Research (CEFIPRA/IFCPAR) for the [ADEPT](https://ifindia.cdn.betanet.in/ifindia/uploads/2024/03/PhD-in-AI-Computer-Science.pdf) project.
